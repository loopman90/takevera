# Community code of conduct

This project adopts the principles of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/): participate respectfully, welcome different perspectives, give constructive feedback, respect privacy and take responsibility for the impact of your actions. Harassment, discrimination, unwanted sexual attention, threats and publishing another person's private information without permission are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove or edit inappropriate contributions, request changes, limit interactions or suspend participation in proportion to the conduct and its impact. They should act consistently, document decisions privately where appropriate and offer a way to request review.

A private enforcement contact has not yet been established. Before opening the community for public contributions, maintainers must provide a confidential reporting route and publish it here. Do not post personal or sensitive details in a public issue.
